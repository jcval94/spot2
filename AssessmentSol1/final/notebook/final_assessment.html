5. Opportunity Score: integration without double counting

After recovery, Lead Quality itself contains selected-Spot matching context. Multiplying continuous Inventory Serviceability again degraded pure Lead Quality ranking, even while improving exact-serviceable joint concentration.

Source: AssessmentSol1/recovery_downstream/CAPACITY_REEVALUATION.csv · top15 diagnostic

Therefore the final score is:

Opportunity Score V2 = lead_quality_probability × inventory_actionability_gate

The continuous Inventory Serviceability score remains separate. This is not a jointly calibrated probability.

Operating rule. Use Lead Quality to maximize scheduled-visit progression regardless of inventory. Use Opportunity Score when progression and serviceability both matter.

8. Limitations

  • June procedural holdout is non-pristine and diagnostic-only.
  • Top-5 Lead Quality Lift remains below 1.
  • Recovery uncertainty is wide.
  • Spot prices are unversioned historically, so precise budget fit is blocked/unknown.
  • Real score ties require rank-based priority bands.
  • No causal or commercial-conversion claim is supported.
  • Opportunity Score is not a jointly calibrated probability.
  • LLM human precision/recall is unavailable.

9. Final recommendation

Operate the T1 post-recovery system at P80/top20, with K=3 fallback and Opportunity Score V2 for serviceability-aware prioritization. Preserve Lead Quality separately for use cases where inventory should not constrain progression prioritization.
